# Captain Preferences

- **astro-miner vision**: 2D topdown space shooter idler (Asteroids-inspired mining company). Player manages an asteroid mining operation, purchasing autonomous mining ships and upgrades to mine increasingly large asteroids.
- **astro-miner core mechanics**: Asteroids remain stationary in the center, while autonomous player ships navigate and mine them. Asteroids are constructed of square grid blocks in a circular shape, with each individual block possessing its own HP.
- **astro-miner ship & camera behavior**: Camera smoothly follows the player ship. Ships face the direction of flight with a glowing engine trail behind them, seeking out and staying close to damaged blocks to destroy them first before moving on.
